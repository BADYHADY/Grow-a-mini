## Phase gates

- **Phase 1 → 2:** five minutes of watching your Mini fight is fun.
- **Phase 2 → 3:** hitting a basic enemy feels satisfying on a phone.
- **Phase 3 → 4:** five testers with zero explanation can describe the game afterwards.
- **Phase 5 → 6:** D7 ≥ 17 % before a single Robux product is built.
